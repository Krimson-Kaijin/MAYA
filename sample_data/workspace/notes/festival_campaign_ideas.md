# Festival Season Campaign — Working Notes

Rough ideas for the Dussehra–Diwali logistics surge campaign. Not reviewed yet.

## Positioning

Pitch Dhruva as the carrier that treats festival deadlines as sacred. Last year we
delivered 97 percent of festival consignments on time while the industry average
slid to 84 percent. That gap is the campaign.

## Ideas

1. "Pandagaku Time-ki" (on time for the festival) — a guarantee program for
   registered clients: festival-window consignments get priority allocation and an
   automatic escalation lane. Needs ops sign-off on capacity.
2. Client webinar in early September on festival documentation readiness — e-way
   bill checklists, packaging standards for sweets and electronics, cut-off dates.
3. A festival cut-off calendar as a downloadable PDF and WhatsApp broadcast.
4. Small gesture: personalised Diwali greetings with each delivered consignment in
   the festival window. Cheap, memorable.
