Dhruva Freight — Q2 2026 Vendor Performance Review
Prepared by: Operations Analytics · Date: 2026-07-02

Summary

Across April to June we moved 4,812 consignments through six contracted carriers.
On-time performance averaged 91.4 percent, up from 88.9 percent in Q1. Two vendors
fell below the 90 percent contractual threshold: Sagar Roadlines at 86.2 percent
and Eastern Cargo at 88.1 percent. Krishna Transport remained the strongest partner
at 96.8 percent on-time with the lowest damage rate in the network.

Detail

Sagar Roadlines' decline concentrates on the Hyderabad to Chennai lane, where
driver shortages caused fourteen missed pickups in May alone. Their management has
proposed a dedicated fleet arrangement from August; the proposal is commercially
attractive but their maintenance backlog is a concern. Eastern Cargo's issues trace
to the Visakhapatnam port runs, where documentation errors caused repeated gate
rejections. A joint documentation workshop was held on June 25 and early July
numbers show improvement.

Damage claims for the quarter totalled eleven incidents, of which seven involved
fragile electronics on the Vijayawada lane. Packaging audit findings suggest the
issue is inadequate corner protection rather than handling. Krishna Transport has
agreed to a packaging pilot with foam-in-place at no additional cost through
September.

Risks

There is a material risk that Sagar Roadlines misses the Q3 threshold again, which
would trigger the contract's step-down clause and force a 15 percent volume
reallocation at peak festival season. Eastern Cargo's improvement is fragile and
depends on one senior documentation clerk. Concentrating more volume on Krishna
Transport increases single-vendor dependency beyond the 40 percent policy ceiling.

Recommendations

1. Place Sagar Roadlines on a formal performance improvement plan with weekly
   reviews through August, with the dedicated fleet decision deferred until they
   hold 90 percent for six consecutive weeks.
2. Fund a second trained documentation clerk for Eastern Cargo's Visakhapatnam
   desk before the festival surge.
3. Extend the Krishna Transport packaging pilot to all electronics consignments
   and re-baseline damage targets in October.
