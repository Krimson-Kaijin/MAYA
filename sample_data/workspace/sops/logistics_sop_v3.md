# Dhruva Freight — Logistics Standard Operating Procedure (v3)

Effective date: 2026-06-20 · Owner: Operations (Ravi Teja) · Supersedes: v2 (2026-02-11)

## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the end-to-end handling procedure for outbound shipments from the
Hyderabad and Vijayawada hubs, including booking, documentation, carrier handoff,
exception handling, and proof-of-delivery closure. Version 3 introduces the new
exception escalation matrix and reduces the documentation turnaround target from
24 hours to 12 hours.

## 2. Booking window

All outbound bookings must be entered into the TMS before 15:00 IST for same-day
carrier allocation. Bookings received after 15:00 IST roll to the next working day
unless flagged as priority by the client services team. Priority bookings require
approval from the hub supervisor and must include the client PO reference.

## 3. Documentation

Every shipment requires: commercial invoice, e-way bill, packing list, and the
carrier consignment note. The documentation team must complete verification within
12 hours of booking. Missing e-way bills are the single largest cause of hub delays
and must be escalated to the client on the same day.

## 4. Carrier handoff

Carrier pickup must be confirmed in the TMS with photo proof of sealed loading.
Drivers must present a valid consignment note; unmatched vehicles are refused at
the gate. The dock supervisor should reconcile the manifest against loaded cartons
before release.

## 5. Exception escalation matrix (new in v3)

- Level 1 (delay under 6 hours): hub coordinator notifies client services.
- Level 2 (delay 6–24 hours): supervisor calls the client; recovery plan logged.
- Level 3 (delay over 24 hours or damage): operations head and client services
  director must be informed within one hour; incident report due within 48 hours.

## 6. Proof of delivery

POD must be uploaded within 24 hours of delivery. Unclosed PODs older than 72 hours
appear on the weekly exceptions report and need written closure notes. Repeated POD
delays by a carrier trigger a quarterly scorecard review.
