# Dhruva Freight — Logistics Standard Operating Procedure (v2)

Effective date: 2026-02-11 · Owner: Operations (Ravi Teja) · Supersedes: v1 (2025-08-01)

## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the outbound shipment handling procedure for the Hyderabad hub,
covering booking, documentation, carrier handoff, and proof-of-delivery closure.

## 2. Booking window

All outbound bookings must be entered into the TMS before 14:00 IST for same-day
carrier allocation. Bookings received after 14:00 IST roll to the next working day.

## 3. Documentation

Every shipment requires: commercial invoice, e-way bill, packing list, and the
carrier consignment note. The documentation team must complete verification within
24 hours of booking.

## 4. Carrier handoff

Carrier pickup must be confirmed in the TMS. Drivers must present a valid
consignment note. The dock supervisor reconciles the manifest before release.

## 5. Exceptions

Delays should be reported to the hub coordinator, who informs client services by
end of day. Damage cases go to the operations head within 24 hours.

## 6. Proof of delivery

POD must be uploaded within 48 hours of delivery. Unclosed PODs older than one week
appear on the monthly exceptions report.

## 7. Review cadence

This SOP is reviewed twice a year. Next review is due by 2026-08-11.
